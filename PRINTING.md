# Printing Instructions

## Full-Page Version
Download `Thanksgiving_Coloring_Book_Complete.pdf` and print single- or double-sided on letter/A4 paper.

## Booklet Version (Recommended for pocket size)
1. Download `Thanksgiving_Coloring_Book_BOOKLET.pdf`
2. Print **double-sided** (flip on the short edge / landscape)
3. Nest the sheets in the order they appear in the PDF
4. Fold in half along the center
5. Staple 2–3 times along the fold (saddle stitch)

You will get a neat half-letter (approx 5.5" × 8.5") coloring booklet.
