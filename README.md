# The Thanksgiving Coloring Book – History & Scripture Edition

A high-quality, printable black-and-white coloring book telling the story of the First Thanksgiving (1621) woven with biblical scriptures of gratitude.

**Same style as the Nativity Coloring Book** — clean thick black outlines, ornate decorative borders, full scripture quotes, short story summaries, and original Hebrew/Greek language notes.

## Printable PDFs

- `Thanksgiving_Coloring_Book_Complete.pdf` — Full-page version (7 pages)  
- `Thanksgiving_Coloring_Book_BOOKLET.pdf` — Booklet-imposed version for saddle-stitch / fold & staple printing  

## How to Print

1. Download the desired PDF or individual JPG pages.  
2. Print on letter or A4 paper (cardstock recommended).  
3. For the booklet PDF: print **double-sided** (flip on short edge), nest the sheets, fold in half, and staple in the center.  
4. Color and give thanks!

## Language Notes

- Hebrew: *todah* / *yadah* = thanksgiving / praise  
- Greek: *eucharistia* / *eucharisteo* = thanksgiving / to give thanks
